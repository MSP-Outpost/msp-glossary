# Contributing

Help us keep these definitions clear and correct.

## Suggest a Term

Open an issue with the Suggest a Term form. New terms are published on [mspoutpost.com/glossary](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary) first, then added here with a link to their page.

## Fix a Definition

Open a Correction issue, or a pull request that updates both `README.md` and `glossary.json`.

## Style

- One line per definition, in plain English.
- Keep terms in alphabetical order under the right letter.
- Link each term to its MSP Outpost page.

By contributing you agree to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and to license your contribution under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE).
