# MSP Glossary

> Plain English definitions of the terms MSPs use every day, from the [MSP Outpost glossary](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary).

This glossary holds 44 terms. Each one links to its full page on MSP Outpost, with examples and common questions. The same data is available as [glossary.json](glossary.json).

## A

- **[AISP (All-In Seat Price)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/aisp)**: The average monthly recurring revenue an MSP earns per supported user seat, across every managed services client. It folds all recurring services into one per-user figure.
- **[AR (Accounts Receivable)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/accounts-receivable)**: Money clients owe you for work or services already invoiced and not yet paid. It sits on the balance sheet as an asset until it is collected.
- **[AYCE (All You Can Eat)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/ayce)**: An all-you-can-eat agreement bundles unlimited support for a fixed monthly fee, with no per-ticket or per-hour billing for work inside its scope.

## B

- **[BAA (Business Associate Agreement)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/baa)**: A business associate agreement is the contract a healthcare client must have with you before you touch anything containing patient information, setting out what you may do with it and what you owe them if it leaks.
- **[Billable Utilization](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/billable-utilization)**: The percentage of a technician's available working hours that are logged against client work. Available hours exclude holidays and approved leave.
- **[BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/byod)**: BYOD is an arrangement where staff use personal phones, tablets or laptops to do company work, rather than being issued equipment the business owns.

## C

- **[CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/cac)**: The total sales and marketing spend in a period divided by the number of new clients won in it. It is what one new client actually cost you to win.
- **[CASL](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/casl)**: CASL means Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation. It regulates commercial electronic messages and also covers activities such as installing computer programs, so an MSP should check both its marketing practices and its software deployment arrangements.
- **[CMMC](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/cmmc)**: CMMC is the American defense department's framework for proving that a contractor handling sensitive but unclassified government information has the security controls it claims, verified at intervals rather than asserted.
- **[Co-managed IT](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/co-managed)**: Co-managed IT is an arrangement where an MSP works alongside a client's internal IT staff rather than replacing them, with responsibilities split by agreement rather than by default.
- **[CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/csat)**: The share of post-ticket survey responses that rate the support experience positively. It measures how clients felt about individual interactions with the service desk.

## D

- **[DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/dso)**: The average number of days between sending an invoice and receiving payment. It measures how quickly billed revenue turns into cash.
- **[Dwell Time](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/dwell-time)**: How long an attacker is inside an environment before being detected. It runs from the first compromise to the moment someone notices it.

## E

- **[EBITDA](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/ebitda)**: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. It approximates the operating profit a business produces, independent of how it is financed or taxed.
- **[EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/edr)**: EDR is software on endpoints that watches process and system behavior, records it, and allows an operator to investigate and respond (isolating a machine or killing a process) rather than only blocking known-bad files.
- **[eNPS (Employee Net Promoter Score)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/enps)**: The NPS question asked of your own staff: how likely are you to recommend this company as a place to work. Promoters minus detractors, from minus 100 to plus 100.

## F

- **[FCR (First Contact Resolution)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/fcr)**: First contact resolution is the share of tickets resolved in the initial interaction, without escalation, a callback, or a second touch.

## G

- **[Gross Margin](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/gross-margin)**: Revenue minus the direct cost of delivering it, such as technician labor, tools and resold product, expressed as a percentage of revenue.
- **[GRR (Gross Revenue Retention)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/grr)**: The share of last year's recurring revenue from existing clients that you kept, counting cancellations and downgrades but ignoring any expansion. It can never exceed 100 percent.

## H

- **[HIPAA](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/hipaa)**: HIPAA is the American health-privacy law that decides how patient information must be protected, and it reaches you directly the moment you provide IT services to anyone who holds that information.

## I

- **[ITIL](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/itil)**: ITIL is a published framework of IT service management practices (incident, problem, change and service request handling among them), used as a common vocabulary and a starting structure.
- **[ITSM (IT Service Management)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/itsm)**: IT service management is the practice of running IT as a set of defined services with owners, processes and agreed levels, rather than as a queue of requests handled however whoever picks them up prefers.

## L

- **[Logo Churn](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/logo-churn)**: The number of clients who ended their agreement in a period, as a percentage of the clients you had at the start of it. It counts clients, not dollars.
- **[LTV (Lifetime Value)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/ltv)**: The total gross margin a typical client produces over the whole time they stay with you. It is usually estimated as monthly gross margin per client times average client lifetime in months.

## M

- **[MDR (Managed Detection and Response)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/mdr)**: MDR is detection and response delivered as a service, where an external team monitors the tooling, triages what it produces, and responds, rather than the software being handed over for someone in-house to watch.
- **[MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/mfa)**: A sign-in that needs two or more kinds of proof, such as a password plus a code from an app or a hardware key. A stolen password alone is then not enough to get in.
- **[MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/mrr)**: Monthly recurring revenue is the predictable, contracted revenue an MSP bills every month, counted separately from project work, hardware resale and hourly labor.

## N

- **[NPS (Net Promoter Score)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/nps)**: A loyalty score from one question: how likely are you to recommend us, on a scale of 0 to 10. It is the percentage of promoters (9 or 10) minus the percentage of detractors (0 to 6).
- **[NRR (Net Revenue Retention)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/nrr)**: The recurring revenue your existing clients pay today as a percentage of what the same clients paid a year ago, counting expansion, price increases, downgrades and cancellations.

## P

- **[PHIPA](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/phipa)**: PHIPA is Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act. It governs personal health information handled by health information custodians and sets obligations for their agents and electronic service providers.
- **[PIPEDA](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/pipeda)**: PIPEDA is Canada's federal private-sector privacy law for personal information handled in commercial activities. Its full name is the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act. Provincial and sector-specific laws affect which rules apply.
- **[PSA (Professional Services Automation)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/psa)**: PSA is the system an MSP runs its business in: tickets, time entry, contracts, billing and reporting, ideally connected to the tooling that generates the work.

## Q

- **[QBR (Quarterly Business Review)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/qbr)**: A quarterly business review is a scheduled meeting between an MSP and a client to review service delivery, discuss the client's business direction, and plan the technology work that follows from it.
- **[Quebec Law 25](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/quebec-law-25)**: Quebec Law 25 is a privacy reform that amended Quebec's personal-information laws. For private businesses, the operative duties sit in the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector as amended.

## R

- **[RMM (Remote Monitoring and Management)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/rmm)**: RMM is the software an MSP uses to monitor client endpoints and servers, apply patches, run scripts and access machines remotely, usually through an agent installed on each device.
- **[RPO (Recovery Point Objective)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/rpo)**: The most data, measured in time, the business can afford to lose in a failure. An RPO of four hours means backups must run at least every four hours.
- **[RTO (Recovery Time Objective)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/rto)**: The longest a system can be down after a failure before the outage becomes unacceptable to the business. It sets how fast a restore has to be.

## S

- **[SLA (Service Level Agreement)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/sla)**: A service level agreement is the part of a contract that states what response and resolution a client is entitled to, usually by severity, and what happens when you miss it.
- **[SOC 2](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/soc-2)**: SOC 2 is an audit report in which an independent firm examines how you handle security, availability and confidentiality, and states whether your controls did what you said they did.

## T

- **[TTFR (Time to First Response)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/ttfr)**: Time to first response is how long a client waits between raising a ticket and hearing from a human who has read it.
- **[TTR (Time to Resolution)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/ttr)**: Time to resolution is how long a ticket takes from being raised to the underlying issue being fixed, measured end to end rather than by how long anyone worked on it.

## V

- **[vCIO (Virtual CIO)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/vcio)**: A vCIO is someone who provides technology strategy to a client that has no CIO of its own: planning, budgeting, risk and roadmap rather than support and remediation.

## X

- **[XDR (Extended Detection and Response)](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/xdr)**: XDR extends endpoint detection by correlating signals from other layers (identity, email, cloud services, network) so that related events are assessed together rather than as separate alerts.

## Z

- **[Zero Trust](https://mspoutpost.com/glossary/zero-trust)**: Zero trust is a security approach that verifies every request on its own merits (who is asking, from what device, for what) instead of granting access because the request came from inside the network.
